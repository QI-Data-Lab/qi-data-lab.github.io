The idea behind our work

Quality infrastructure connects measurements, testing, standards, conformity assessment and accreditation. We explore how its evidence can become machine-readable, traceable, interoperable and verifiable across organizations and products.

Our research strategy

Three pillars. One direction.

Agentic quality infrastructure needs more than AI. It needs information with shared meaning, trusted systems that can exchange it, and AI workflows that can justify their actions. Our research connects all three.

PILLAR / 01

Digital documents & vocabularies

Make quality information understandable to software, with well-defined data models, common terms, and machine-checkable rules.

  • Digital reference material documents (DRMD) and certificates
  • Metrology vocabularies, units and semantics
  • Machine-actionable standards and validation
Explore DRMDSmart Standards
PILLAR / 02

Infrastructure

Build the interoperability and trust layer so digital evidence can move across assets, organizations and the product lifecycle.

  • Asset Administration Shells (AAS) and digital twins
  • Digital product and material passports (DPP)
  • Federated data spaces, Quality-X and verifiable credentials
Explore VC4QIQuality-X context
PILLAR / 03

AI

Investigate AI-assisted quality workflows that can interpret evidence while preserving provenance, checks and human oversight.

  • Document understanding and structured extraction
  • Evidence-grounded retrieval and source tracing
  • Agent orchestration, validation and human review
Explore Evidence Tracer
Research goalAgentic QI

Quality workflows where software agents can discover, interpret, verify and exchange evidence across systems—with explicit provenance, policy checks and accountable human control. This is our research direction, not a claim of a deployed autonomous service.

How we approach the work

Research that can be inspected, reused and challenged.
