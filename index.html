The idea behind our work

Quality infrastructure connects measurements, testing, standards, conformity assessment and accreditation. We explore how its evidence can become machine-readable, traceable, interoperable and verifiable across organizations and products.

Research areas

The digital building blocks of trust.

Our work spans data models, software, trust mechanisms and AI-assisted workflows. We connect these pieces so quality information remains useful beyond its original document or system.

FIELD / A

Machine-readable quality documents

Structured digital certificates and reference material documents, with reusable schemas, validation rules and links to standards and metrology vocabularies.

FIELD / B

Verifiable credentials & trust

Representing quality claims as verifiable evidence—and evaluating the issuer’s authority, scope, status and supporting trust chain.

FIELD / C

Interoperability & data ecosystems

Connecting quality data through Asset Administration Shells, federated data-space concepts such as Quality-X, and digital product passports.

FIELD / D

Evidence-grounded AI

Document understanding, information extraction and agentic review workflows that keep answers linked to inspectable source material.

How we approach the work

Research that can be inspected, reused and challenged.
